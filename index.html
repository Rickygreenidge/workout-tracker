How to progress

When you reach the top of an exercise's rep range at the target RPE, increase the load next time and build back up from the lower end.

Movement visuals are simplified anatomical schematics, not technical form demonstrations. Use them to identify the exercise; prioritize your gym's equipment instructions and sound technique. RPE 10 represents failure; the program does not prescribe failure training.
